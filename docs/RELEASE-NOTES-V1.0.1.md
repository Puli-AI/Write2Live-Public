# Write2Live v1.0.1

Released to the controlled-access service on 13 September 2026.

## What changed

- A completed attempt now leads to **Start a fresh session** as the primary next step.
- **Continue this writing** remains an explicit option for revision or extension.
- Unfinished browser-local writing still resumes, protecting work that was not completed.
- When no sessions remain, the workspace routes the writer to request more sessions.
- The administrator workspace begins with live counts and links for both request queues.

## What did not change

- AI actions remain optional and occur only when the writer asks.
- AI scores remain coaching indicators, not standardized examination grades.
- Administrator progress reporting remains aggregate-only; it does not reveal writing content or AI feedback.
- The release does not add educator or classroom access to student writing.

## Next design question

A future, writer-owned session-review page may bring the original input, refinement, scores, strengths, growth areas, suggestions, exercises, and next focus together. It must enforce server-side ownership. Any later educator view will require explicit consent, scoped roles, auditability, and retention rules.

The screenshots in this repository use an anonymized demo identity. Images showing real administrator records are not published.
