# Platform direction

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14

Write2Live is planned as more than a single focused-writing screen. Puli Consulting intends it to become the common learning core for a family of services that help people develop independent writing over time.

This document describes direction, not currently available features or confirmed release dates.

## Shared promise

> Help a learner turn evidence and imperfect thoughts into demonstrably better independent writing over time.

The learner should remain the author. AI can support discovery, planning, questioning, feedback, revision, and reflection, but should not quietly replace the learner's thinking or writing.

Across the planned services, the learning loop remains:

**orient → gather or choose evidence → write → receive feedback → revise → practise → demonstrate transfer**

## Planned service suites

| Suite | Intended users | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Academic research and writing | MBA, EMBA, DBA, PhD, undergraduate, and postgraduate learners | Guide research framing, evidence use, argument development, staged writing, revision, provenance, and supervisor-ready checkpoints. |
| Syllabus-aligned English learning | Initially HKDSE students and teachers, with later regional expansion | Combine authentic tasks, educator-reviewed rubrics, repeated practice, and honest progress evidence. |
| Writing games | Different ages and learner profiles | Use short, targeted practice to improve fluency and quality, followed by reflection, retry, and transfer to real writing. |

All three suites are intended to use shared identity, consent, protected drafting, learner-owned history, feedback records, progress, data controls, and evaluation standards. Syllabus-specific and discipline-specific behavior should remain in reviewed, versioned learning packs rather than changing the meaning of the core product for everyone.

## Academic direction

The first academic pilots should emphasize applied research for MBA, EMBA, and DBA learners. This is a strong fit for structured research intake, evidence ledgers, source locators, claim checking, and staged publication-quality review. Broader undergraduate and postgraduate uses can follow. PhD support requires later, stronger disciplinary, supervisor, citation, and research-integrity safeguards.

The service should guide a learner from a defensible research question to a well-supported submission while preserving authorship. It should show where evidence came from, which claim it supports, what changed between attempts, and what decisions remain the learner's responsibility.

Selected research capabilities from Puli StrategyLab may support this suite through controlled, versioned integrations. Write2Live and StrategyLab remain separate products; this direction does not imply a merger of their applications or data.

## HKDSE and regional direction

Puli Consulting plans to work with Hong Kong Beacon on an educator-reviewed HKDSE English practice suite. The intended division is to combine Puli Consulting's product engineering, security, operations, and evaluation infrastructure with Beacon's curriculum, teacher, marker, and student-pilot expertise.

Any HKDSE offer should:

- use authentic contexts, audiences, purposes, registers, and text types;
- support drafting and revision rather than only one-shot scoring;
- use versioned, educator-reviewed tasks and rubrics;
- compare AI feedback with independently marked work;
- protect children and learner data with age-appropriate controls; and
- avoid implying HKEAA endorsement or predicted examination levels without evidence.

Expansion to another region should require a new local syllabus pack, local educator review, a representative benchmark, and locally valid language for interpreting progress.

## Writing-game direction

Games should be small learning interventions, not a separate arcade and not a reason to maximize screen time. Each game should target an observable skill, preserve the learner's work, provide safe pressure settings, end with reflection or retry, and test transfer to a later authentic task.

Initial candidates include openings, concision, register switching, evidence use, cohesion, argument development, and endings. Early games should be tested within the educator-reviewed HKDSE work before adaptation for other age groups and contexts.

## How Write2Live intends to compete

Write2Live does not need to imitate every feature of a mature blocker, editor, habit tracker, academic assistant, or research-review product. Its intended advantage is the governed connection between focused writing, evidence, coaching, targeted practice, and demonstrated progress.

The long-term differentiators are:

1. a visible history of the learner's own attempts, decisions, revisions, and reflections;
2. traceability from evidence to claim to revision;
3. syllabus and domain packs validated with qualified educators and partners;
4. games that lead to improvement on later real writing; and
5. partner workflows that save teacher or supervisor time while protecting learner agency.

## Measures that matter

Future product decisions should be judged using learning and trust evidence, including:

- first-draft completion and repeat use;
- improvement between attempts and on later transfer tasks;
- human-marker agreement and feedback stability;
- whether suggestions are understood and applied;
- evidence-to-claim coverage and fewer unsupported or citation errors;
- teacher or supervisor time saved;
- learner control and responsible AI use; and
- provider cost, support load, accessibility, safety, and reliability.

Public claims will remain limited until the relevant partner review, benchmark, privacy, accessibility, and safety gates have been met.
