# The story behind Write2Live

Write2Live did not begin as a generic AI-writing product. It began with Dr. M.K. Mak's own difficulty producing enough first-draft quantity for careful revision to improve.

## From Write-or-Die to Write2Live

The original [Write-or-Die repository](https://github.com/DrMakMoonKuen/Write-or-Die) remains public as the historical prototype. Write2Live is the current controlled-access service from Puli Consulting. It is inspired by the broader writing-pressure category but is not affiliated with the original Write or Die product.

The change of name reflects the intended purpose: help an idea live long enough to be written, revised, understood, and shared.
