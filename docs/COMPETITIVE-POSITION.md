# Competitive position

Last reviewed: 2026-09-13

Write2Live combines capabilities that are often separated across writing products: current-topic discovery, a configurable momentum session, after-writing AI assistance, practical exercises, and personal progress.

## Where it fits

| Product category | Examples | Their documented strength | Write2Live's choice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Distraction and pressure tools | [Cold Turkey Writer](https://getcoldturkey.com/writer/features/), [Squibler Dangerous Writing App](https://www.squibler.io/dangerous-writing-prompt-app) | Strong constraints that force continued writing. | Offer chosen pressure without making loss of work the default. |
| Habit tools | [750 Words](https://site.750words.com/) | Daily repetition, streaks, and personal statistics. | Add skill-specific small games after validating the core coaching loop. |
| Learning feedback | [Cambridge Write & Improve](https://writeandimprove.com/) | Repeated writing, feedback, and level-oriented progress. | Build an educator-marked benchmark before making standardized-level claims. |
| General editing assistants | [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/features), [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) | Broad real-time correction and extensive editing reports. | Protect uninterrupted composition; bring AI in only when the writer asks after writing. |
| AI-native editors | [Lex](https://lex.page/) | Document creation, collaboration, versions, and publishing. | Keep collaboration as a later educator-workflow need rather than a v1 goal. |

## Current advantage

The differentiated workflow is **discover → write without interruption → reflect → practise again**. Web-grounded topic discovery and chosen momentum modes make the blank-page stage easier; after-writing feedback preserves the writer's responsibility for composing.

## Current limitations

- AI scores have not been externally calibrated and must be read as coaching indicators.
- Habit and revision loops are still limited.
- Collaboration and classroom assignment workflows are not present.
- Self-service export/deletion and formal accessibility evidence remain roadmap work.
- The product is not an official HKDSE assessment service.

The detailed evaluation protocol is maintained in the private product repository. Public claims will be updated only when supporting evidence is available.
